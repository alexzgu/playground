**9.2. Metropolis Algorithms**

The precise algorithm King Markov used is a special case of the general METROPOLIS ALGORITHM from the real world.[^139] And this algorithm is an example of Markov chain Monte Carlo. In real applications, the goal is of course not to help an autocrat schedule his journeys, but instead to draw samples from an unknown and usually complex target distribution, like a posterior probability distribution.

- The "islands" in our objective are parameter values, and they need not be discrete, but can instead take on a continuous range of values as usual.
- The "population sizes" in our objective are the posterior probabilities at each parameter value.
- The "weeks" in our objective are samples taken from the joint posterior of the parameters in the model.
