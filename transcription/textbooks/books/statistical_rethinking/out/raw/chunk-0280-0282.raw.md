**8.4. Summary**

This chapter introduced *interactions*, which allow for the association between a predictor and an outcome to depend upon the value of another predictor. While you can't see them in a DAG, interactions can be important for making accurate inferences. Interactions can be difficult to interpret, and so the chapter also introduced *triptych* plots that help in visualizing the effect of an interaction.
